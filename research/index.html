Research

The Boulder Optical Laboratory for Translational Science (BOLTS) pioneers optical imaging technologies that surpass current limits in speed, accuracy, and accessibility, advancing translational research. Our work spans five interconnected areas, from laser-induced ultrasound and entangled photons to ultrafast cameras and machine learning.

Photoacoustic Imaging

Using laser-induced ultrasonic waves to create detailed images of the inside of the body, photoacoustic imaging combines optical contrast with acoustic penetration depth, enabling noninvasive, rapid imaging of hemodynamics in humans.

Key papers: Nat. Biomed. Eng. 2023, Nat. Biomed. Eng. 2025, Nat. Biomed. Eng. 2022, Nat. Photon. 2022, Nat. Protoc. 2021, Sci. Adv. 2024, Light Sci. Appl. 2023

Quantum Imaging

Employing spatially and polarization-entangled photon pairs to generate images with enhanced resolution and sensitivity, overcoming challenges such as poor signal-to-noise ratios and low resolvable pixel counts.

Key papers: Sci. Adv. 2024, Nat. Commun. 2023

Ultrafast Imaging

Visualizing rapid biological and physical processes with extremely high temporal resolution — from passive current flows in myelinated axons to electromagnetic pulses propagating in dielectrics.

Key papers: Nat. Commun. 2022, Nat. Photon. 2023, Light Sci. Appl. 2023

Fluorescence Imaging

Capturing fluorescence intensity and lifetime to analyze biological structures and functions, including real-time multiphoton fluorescence lifetime imaging microscopy (FLIM) and super-resolution microscopy.

Key papers: Optica 2021, Light Sci. Appl. 2023

Machine Learning

Utilizing AI/ML algorithms to analyze and enhance biomedical images for better diagnosis, including the first Poisson–Gaussian denoising dataset for benchmarking image denoising algorithms in high-quality, real-time biomedical applications.

Key papers: CVPR 2019, Optica 2022
